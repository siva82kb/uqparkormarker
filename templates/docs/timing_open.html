Two clocks

The timestamps come from the clock of the computer that recorded the video. Each sensor system keeps its own time. Two clocks never agree exactly, and they slowly move apart.

Example. Two wristwatches. One is 2.0 s ahead of the other in the morning and 2.3 s ahead by the evening.

The 2.0 s is the offset. The extra 0.3 s over the day is the drift. Knowing both, a time on one watch can be turned into a time on the other.

How the jumps tie the clocks together

The landings also cancel the fixed delay between filming and stamping, because that delay is the same in the jump videos as in every other video.

This step has not been done. It is not part of this app.

What this app hands over

  • Each clip's video file and its first and last frame number. These are exact.
  • The timestamps of those two frames, as recorded, in local time.
  • Only clips with no gap and no repeated timestamp inside them.

What it leaves as it is

  • A timestamp can be one frame off, about 30 ms.
  • Every timestamp is taken a little after the frame was filmed. This fixed delay has not been measured.
  • Clip times are on the video computer's clock, not a sensor's.

Questions still open

  • Do the sensors share the video computer's clock? If they do, the jumps are only a check. If they do not, the jumps are essential. This is not yet known.
  • Are the jump videos themselves clean? In most participants one or both jump videos contain one to three repeated timestamps or gaps. Landings should be read from parts of those videos where the timestamps are normal.
  • Did the study's recording program stamp frames as the published one does? The study videos are at 30 frames a second and the published program records at 10, so a changed version was used. Its code has not been seen.
  • What caused the hold-ups of many seconds? The frame rate explains scattered repeats. It does not explain these, and they differ from session to session.
  • Should late timestamps be corrected instead of removed? That would bring back the lost footage and tasks. It needs a method that is agreed and validated first.
  • Other participants. Only the least affected one is used so far. The others lose more footage and more tasks under the same rule.
